CyberGrill 15 - keeping a secret, and a better hand of cards
============================================================

THIS ONE NEEDS A SERVER DEPLOY. The Worker changed (it now tailors what each
player is told), so after update.ps1:

    cd server
    npx wrangler deploy
    cd ..

Or run deploy15.ps1, which does the whole lot.

THE RONDA YOU WERE GIVING AWAY
  When you had a ronda or a tringa, everybody else's screen said "AMIN IS
  DECIDING WHETHER TO CALL..." - which told the whole table exactly what you
  were holding. That is the one thing the game lets you keep to yourself, and
  the app was announcing it.

  Three leaks, all closed:
    * The turn pointer. During the calling phase it names the one player at
      the table holding a pair, so it is now hidden from everybody except the
      person being asked. Your own prompt comes from your own hand, not from
      the pointer. Nobody's seat lights up.
    * The message. It reads "THE TABLE IS BEING ASKED..." now, with no name.
    * "AMIN says nothing." That line went to everyone, which announced the
      pair just as loudly as calling it would have. The server now runs the
      events through a filter once per player before sending them, and that
      line only ever reaches the person it is about.

  The same filter runs against the bots, so a bot sitting on a pair keeps it
  from you too.

THE SCOREBOARD IS AN ADMIN'S JOB AGAIN
  Online results no longer file themselves onto a locked board. They wait in
  the tray - nothing is lost - and the moment somebody unlocks with the
  passcode they all go in at once. A locked board tells you how many are
  waiting.

PLAYING A CARD
  The card you played used to stay in your hand while a copy of it flew to
  the table, so you watched your own card in two places at once. The real one
  now disappears the instant the flying one appears. The flight arcs instead
  of sliding, it starts at the size it was in your hand and settles to table
  size, and the cards it lands on visibly take the weight before the sweep
  begins.

DEALING
  There is a deck in the corner of the felt now, with the count on it, and
  the cards are dealt out of it: four to the table and three to every hand at
  the start, three each on every refill, one after another. Nobody can move
  until the deal has landed. On a phone the deck sits below the table rather
  than over the cards.

Verified in a real browser: a two-hander and a four-hander solo, a
three-player online round from the deal to the count, forty-four checks that
the cards on screen match the engine exactly whenever nothing is moving, no
stray animation left behind over a whole round, both languages, a voice room
connecting, results waiting on a locked board and filing on unlock, and a
390px phone. All 366 engine tests pass.
